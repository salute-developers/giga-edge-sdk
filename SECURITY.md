# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Before the first public release, security fixes are provided for the current `main` branch only.
After releases begin, fixes are provided for the latest published GigaEdge SDK release. Older
revisions are not supported; upgrade before reporting an issue that may already be fixed.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue or discussion for a suspected vulnerability. Open the repository's
**Security** tab, select **Report a vulnerability**, and submit a private report containing:

- The affected revision or version and platform.
- Reproduction steps or a proof of concept.
- The expected impact and any known mitigations.
- Whether the issue has been disclosed elsewhere.

Do not include credentials or unrelated sensitive data, and do not test against systems you do not
own or have permission to assess.

Maintainers will evaluate reports as capacity allows and coordinate remediation and disclosure
through the private advisory. No acknowledgement or remediation deadline is guaranteed. Please keep
the report confidential until the maintainers confirm that coordinated disclosure is appropriate.
